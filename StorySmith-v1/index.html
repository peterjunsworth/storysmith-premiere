Welcome to StorySmith

StorySmith helps you process your audio clips for semantic search and AI-powered workflows.

How it works:

  1. Click "Initialize StorySmith" to connect to your project
  2. Select clips from your Premiere Pro project panel
  3. Process selected clips to extract file paths
Step 2: Select all audio clips you will be working with by clicking on 'Load Clips from Project', Select the clips and then "Process Files for StorySmith Assistant"
